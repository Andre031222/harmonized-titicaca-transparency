We thank the Reviewer warmly for the very encouraging final assessment and for
judging the manuscript publishable. We have addressed all five remaining minor
points, as detailed below.

\hrulefill

\textbf{1. Figure 1 --- minor visual polish; ensure black-and-white legibility.}
\resp{Figure~1 now uses the professionally redesigned, high-resolution version
(rounded boxes, consistent typography, clear directional arrows). Because the
diagram follows a single top-to-bottom flow with every stage fully labelled, the
sequence is unambiguous in grayscale/print, where stages are distinguished by
position and text rather than by colour. We will gladly supply a vector version for
production if the Editorial Office requires it.}

\textbf{2. Slight redundancy in the local-recalibration message (Section 5.1 and
Conclusions).}
\resp{We removed the duplication. Section~5.1 now states the extrapolation finding
once and carries a single, emphatic recalibration recommendation at the close of the
subsection; the Conclusions retain only one brief mention. The message is therefore
clear and strong without being repetitive.}

\textbf{3. A few long sentences --- split for readability.}
\resp{We split the long sentence at the end of the framing paragraph in the
Introduction, and the ``targeting'' sentence in Section~5.1, into shorter sentences,
and smoothed adjacent transitions. Content is unchanged.}

\textbf{4. Keywords --- add a term better aligned with the journal.}
\resp{We adopted the Reviewer's suggested keyword set, which now includes
``Environmental monitoring protocol'': Secchi depth; Water transparency;
Environmental monitoring protocol; Sentinel-2; Landsat harmonization; Lake Titicaca;
Random Forest; Conformal prediction; High-altitude lakes.}

\textbf{5. Minor style details.}
\resp{We made the recalibration statement in Section~5.1 more direct (``This is the
single most important condition for safe operational use\ldots''), and sharpened the
final sentence of the Conclusions (``\ldots detect clarity loss early and act before
degradation becomes irreversible---a concrete contribution to the environmental
monitoring and assessment of these transboundary high-altitude freshwater
reserves'').}

\hrulefill

We are very grateful for the Reviewer's thorough and constructive guidance across
all rounds, which has clearly improved the manuscript. We hope the revised version
is now suitable for publication in \textit{Environmental Monitoring and Assessment}.
